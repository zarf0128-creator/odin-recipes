Creamy Garlic Pasta

Description

Creamy garlic pasta is a delicious Italian-inspired dish made with pasta, fresh garlic, butter, cream and parmesan cheese. It is simple to prepare and perfect for a quick meal.

Ingredients

  • 200g pasta
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 cup fresh cream
  • 1/2 cup parmesan cheese
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper
  • Fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Boil water in a large pot and add salt.
  2. Add pasta and cook until tender.
  3. Drain the pasta and keep some pasta water aside.
  4. Heat butter and olive oil in a pan.
  5. Add minced garlic and cook until fragrant.
  6. Pour in the fresh cream and stir well.
  7. Add parmesan cheese, salt and black pepper.
  8. Add the cooked pasta to the sauce.
  9. Mix everything until the pasta is coated evenly.
  10. Garnish with fresh parsley and serve hot.
