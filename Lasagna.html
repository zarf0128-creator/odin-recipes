Classic Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a traditional Italian dish made with layers of pasta, meat sauce, cheese, and delicious seasonings.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna sheets
  • 500g minced meat
  • 2 cups tomato sauce
  • 1 onion
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • Salt and pepper
  • Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Cook the minced meat with chopped onion and garlic.
  3. Add tomato sauce, salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning.
  4. Cook the lasagna sheets according to the instructions.
  5. Spread meat sauce in a baking dish.
  6. Add a layer of lasagna sheets and cheese.
  7. Repeat the layers until all ingredients are used.
  8. Top with mozzarella cheese.
  9. Bake for 30-40 minutes.
  10. Allow it to cool before serving.
