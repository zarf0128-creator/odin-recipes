Homemade Margherita Pizza

Description

Margherita pizza is a classic Italian pizza topped with tomato sauce, fresh mozzarella cheese and basil leaves. It has a crispy crust and a delicious combination of simple ingredients.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3/4 cup warm water
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 1 cup mozzarella cheese
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Oregano to taste

Steps

  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar and salt in a bowl.
  2. Add warm water and olive oil.
  3. Knead the dough until it becomes soft and smooth.
  4. Cover the dough and let it rise for 1 hour.
  5. Preheat the oven to 220°C.
  6. Roll the dough into a round pizza base.
  7. Spread pizza sauce evenly over the base.
  8. Add mozzarella cheese on top.
  9. Bake for 12-15 minutes until the crust is golden.
  10. Add fresh basil leaves and oregano.
  11. Slice and serve hot.
